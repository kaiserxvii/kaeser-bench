# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Kaeser Bench a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or point of view. We value
curiosity, reproducible evidence, thoughtful disagreement, and respect for the people behind both
contributions and evaluations.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas, implementations, and evidence—not people.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest, including model-provider affiliations.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, personal data, or private reports.
- Accept moderation decisions and correction when project standards are not met.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation,
doxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable in any project space. Bad-faith manipulation of benchmark results or provenance is also
incompatible with participation in this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior violates these expectations. Enforcement decisions should consider
context, impact, repeated behavior, and the safety of affected participants.

Until a private conduct-reporting address is published, contact a maintainer privately through
their GitHub profile. Do not use the public issue tracker for sensitive conduct reports. A dedicated
private contact method is required before the repository is announced broadly.
